# What's changed

A running list of what's new in each version, newest first.

## Version 0.1.1 — 30 July 2026

The first public release.

- Talk instead of typing: press two keys, speak, press them again, and your
  words appear in whatever you were working in.
- Two shortcuts — one types your words straight into the app you're using, the
  other puts them on the clipboard so you can paste them yourself.
- Lives in the menu bar on a Mac, the system tray on Windows and Linux.
- Choose your own keyboard shortcuts, your language, and how long it listens
  before stopping on its own.
- See how much of your Deepgram allowance you've used, month by month.
- Keeps a record of everything you've dictated, which you can open from the
  bottom of the app.
- Works on Mac, Windows, and Linux.
